# AI Pipeline

Golden Look must not generate faces, bodies, poses, or clothing types. Gemini assists parsing only; the guardian confirms or edits the result. Modal AI may recolor only known supported clothing regions with known colors.

Planned segmentation model: `mattmdjaga/segformer_b2_clothes`.

Current segmentation model is used for hackathon evaluation/demo purposes. Commercial use must be reviewed before productization and may require replacing the model.
